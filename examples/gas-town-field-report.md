# Two weeks with Gas Town: six workers on a laptop

**Evidence label:** Independent practitioner report  
**By:** Lakshmi Narasimhan  
**Date:** 29 January 2026

**Agents:** 6  
**Reported cost:** Not stated  
**Reported outcome:** Machine overloaded; kept some parts, wrote a simpler tool

## Sources

- [Essay: What Happens When You Let 6 AI Agents Write Code at the Same Time](https://lakshminp.com/2026/01/6-ai-agents-coding-experiment/)

## What it is

A solo developer used Gas Town in its first week on his own projects. Six workers at once left his MacBook running 20+ processes and badly slowed. Orphaned background processes and setup errors took a lot of time. He kept the parts he found solid: Beads task tracking, one worktree per worker, and a hub that dispatches to workers.

## How the agents coordinate

Gas Town's hub and worker model (see the Gas Town entry).

## Setup

Gas Town on an M2 MacBook.

## Limits and caveats

- He says the human still has to dispatch work, so it is not hands-off.
- He concluded two to three workers is practical on a laptop and wrote a simpler tool of his own.
- Written in the first month after release. Gas Town has changed since, and he says some process problems were being fixed.
